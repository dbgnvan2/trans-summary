# BOWEN REFERENCE EXTRACTION

Your task is to find passages where the speaker explicitly attributes a statement, idea, observation, or quote to Murray Bowen / Dr. Bowen by name.

## WHAT TO LOOK FOR

Look for passages where the speaker:
- Directly quotes Bowen: "Bowen said...", "one of my favorite Bowen quotes is...", "to quote Bowen..."
- Attributes an idea or observation to Bowen: "Bowen's key observation that...", "This was Bowen's idea...", "These are all Bowen's ideas", "Bowen's very insightful insight...", "Bowen predicted..."
- Recalls Bowen's words or actions: "I remember Murray saying...", "a tape Murray Bowen did...", "What did Bowen do at NIMH?", "Bowen described...", "Bowen believed..."
- References Bowen's work or thinking: "Bowen was very clear about...", "Bowen's approach was...", "Bowen's switch from..."

## CRITICAL RULE: INCLUDE THE ATTRIBUTION SENTENCE

The extracted quote **MUST** include the specific sentence or phrase that names Bowen as the source.
- If the attribution phrase and the content are in adjacent sentences, include all of them together as one continuous quote.
- Do NOT extract a paraphrase that omits the Bowen attribution. The name "Bowen" (or "Murray") must appear in the quote text itself.

## EXCLUDE

- General references to "Bowen theory" or "Bowen theorists" that don't attribute the statement to Murray Bowen personally
- Statements made by the audience or other speakers that happen to mention Bowen theory
- Pure paraphrases of Bowen concepts where the speaker never names Bowen

## OUTPUT FORMAT

For each reference found, use this format exactly:
> **Concept Descriptor:** "Continuous verbatim quote that includes the attribution naming Bowen."
