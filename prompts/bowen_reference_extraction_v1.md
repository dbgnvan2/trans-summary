# BOWEN REFERENCE EXTRACTION

Extract the passages where the speaker attributes something to **Murray Bowen himself** —
his words, actions, advice, or observations. A Bowen reference is a recollection of the
man, not an exposition of the theory.

## INCLUDE — the speaker recounts Bowen the person
- "Bowen said / wrote / explained / described / emphasized / stressed / suggested / believed / thought …"
- "I remember Dr. Bowen saying …", "Dr. Bowen told me …", "Bowen would say …", "Bowen used to say …"
- "To quote Bowen …", "Bowen's comment was …", "Bowen put it this way …", "Bowen expressed it as …"
- Recollections of Bowen's actions/advice: "Bowen suggested I …", "Bowen had a way of …", "Bowen at NIMH talked about …"

## DO NOT INCLUDE — theory, concepts, or others
- The speaker's own exposition of Bowen-theory concepts (e.g. "Differentiation means …",
  "The emotional system is the force that …", "Triangles are not bad …").
- "Bowen theory …" framing ("Bowen theory says …", "Bowen theory distinguishes …",
  "according to Bowen theory …") — that is the theory, not the man.
- Generic "Bowen theorists" references.
- Content attributed to anyone other than Bowen (e.g. "Kerr said …", "Selye …", "Freud …").

## OUTPUT FORMAT
For each reference found, use the following format exactly:
> **Concept Descriptor:** "Continuous verbatim quote or close paraphrase."
